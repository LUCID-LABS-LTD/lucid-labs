## 7. CONSISTENCY RULES

- [ ] Company name appears as **"LUCID LABS LTD"** in full, identical casing, on every official bio, About section, and page name — never abbreviated to "Lucid Labs" or "LLL" in official fields (short form is fine in running post text)
- [ ] **Do not use the word "Ltd" as a legal claim in formal/registration contexts (e.g., "a registered Nigerian limited company") until the CAC certificate is physically in hand.** Until then, describe the company as "in the process of registration" wherever legal status is implied, even though the name "LUCID LABS LTD" itself can still be used as the brand name across pages.
- [ ] Same amber (`#FFB000`) on near-black (`#0B0A08`) palette on every profile photo, banner, and any graphic posted — no deviation, no platform-specific "let me try a different color for this one"
- [ ] Same one-line description (Section 0) used verbatim anywhere a single description field exists
- [ ] Same tagline — "WE DON'T PROPOSE THE FUTURE. WE BUILD IT." — appears somewhere on every platform (bio, banner, or pinned post)

### 7.1 Dead-page prevention rule
- [ ] **Minimum one post per week, on every active platform, with no exceptions.** A page that goes silent for a month is worse than no page at all — it signals the company folded.

**Five low-effort post ideas for slow weeks (no new hardware milestone needed):**
```
1. Behind-the-scenes photo: your actual workbench/desk mid-project, one sentence
   on what you're currently debugging.
2. A "today I learned" post: one technical lesson from the week, in plain language.
3. Share and comment on a relevant Nigerian tech or robotics news story — add your
   own one-line take, don't just repost silently.
4. A short poll: e.g. "Which should we document next — the sensor calibration
   process or the mobile app build?" — engagement + content research in one.
5. A throwback to the capstone project: one photo/clip from the Sentinel Tank
   build with a short caption tying it to what LUCID LABS is building now.
```
